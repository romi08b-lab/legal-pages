Auto Video Generator

An automated short-form video pipeline, built and run by an independent developer.

Auto Video Generator is a personal software project that produces short, factual "did you know?" videos and publishes them automatically to social media. Every video starts from a single topic and ends as a fully edited, narrated, subtitled clip — with no manual editing step in between.

What it does

Twice a day, the pipeline picks a new topic it hasn't covered before, writes a short script around one surprising, verifiable fact, and turns that script into a finished vertical video: AI-generated narration, synchronized captions, and background footage matched to the topic. The result is uploaded straight to the project's own YouTube and TikTok accounts.

How it works

The pipeline is fully automated, end to end:

  1. Topic selection — a language model proposes a fact-based topic that hasn't been used before, checked against a running history to avoid repeats.
  2. Script writing — a short narration script is drafted, then automatically reviewed for coherence and re-checked against a live web search to confirm the central fact is accurate.
  3. Voice & captions — the script is turned into narration audio, and captions are generated in sync with the voice track.
  4. Footage & assembly — background video clips are selected from stock-footage libraries to match the script, and the whole video is rendered as a single file.
  5. Publishing — the finished video is uploaded to the project's own social accounts, with a title, description, and hashtags generated for each platform.

About this project

This is a personal, non-commercial project: it has a single owner, no public user accounts, and no sign-up or login of any kind. It exists to automate content the owner already wanted to post to their own accounts, not to offer a service to third parties. See the Privacy Policy for details on what data the project handles.
