Privacy Policy — Auto Video Generator

Effective date: August 14, 2026

"Auto Video Generator" is a personal, non-commercial automation project built and operated solely by its owner for their own use. It automatically generates short-form video content and publishes it to the owner's own social media accounts (YouTube, TikTok). This policy explains how it handles data.

No public users, no data collection from the public

This project is not a public-facing website, app, or service. It has no registration, login, or interactive feature available to anyone other than its owner, and it does not collect, process, or store any personal data belonging to any third party or member of the public.

Third-party account credentials

This project uses OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by YouTube (Google) and TikTok, authorized directly by its owner, solely to publish videos to the owner's own accounts on those platforms. These credentials are stored exclusively as encrypted secrets within GitHub Actions and are never transmitted to, shared with, sold to, or made accessible to any other party.

Third-party services used to produce video content

Video scripts and search terms are generated using a third-party AI API (Groq); stock footage is sourced from Pexels; voice narration is generated using Microsoft Edge's text-to-speech service. These calls process the factual subject matter of each video only — never personal data about any individual.

No cookies, tracking, or advertising

Since this project has no public-facing website or app, it does not use cookies, analytics, or advertising technologies of any kind.

This policy may be updated as the project evolves. The current version is always available at this URL.
